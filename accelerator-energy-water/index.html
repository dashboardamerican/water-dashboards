Methodology & sources

Calculation boundary

Facility kWh/M = all-in IT kWh/M × 1.0909 PUE
Direct water = IT kWh/M × 1.15 L/kWh
Grid water = facility kWh/M × 3.14 L/kWh × residual grid share
Effective-work water = physical water ÷ assumed task-efficiency gain
Combined improvement = 2021 full-grid water ÷ effective-work water

The comparison baseline is fixed: the first 2021 accelerator in each family at 0% renewable procurement. Moving the procurement or task-efficiency controls therefore adds those gains to the hardware improvement instead of changing the baseline.

Task-efficiency lever: a scenario for more successful equivalent work per total model token, including fewer retries and more efficient reasoning or agent scaffolds. The dashboard limits this assumption to 1–10×: use 1–4× for conservative matched-task cases and 5–10× for strong workflow gains. This is a sensitivity range, not a measured universal GPT-4-to-current-model trend. OckBench documents much wider token variation at comparable accuracy; METR finds larger capability-horizon gains, which are not themselves per-token estimates. OpenAI reports workload-dependent token savings and recommends evaluating task success alongside total tokens. OpenAI model guidance

Caching and serving: KV/prefix caching, batching, quantization, and speculative decoding reduce computation or improve throughput per raw token; they do not make a token more capable. They remain on the energy-per-token side of this model and are not multiplied again here without workload-specific energy measurements. Published serving results show large throughput effects—PagedAttention/vLLM reports 2–4× and InferCept reports 1.6–2× for interrupted agent workflows—but throughput gains should not be read directly as equal energy savings.

Recent evidence, 2023–2026: no public study establishes one GPT-4-to-current multiplier for useful work per token. OckBench finds GPT-4o averaging 495 decoding tokens at 35% accuracy and GPT-5 averaging 2,336 tokens at 73% on its selected math problems: capability rose sharply, but token use rose too. OckBench paper METR’s May 2026 results similarly show the 50%-success task horizon rising from roughly 3.5 minutes for GPT-4 to more than two hours for GPT-5, but that is a capability measure—not a per-token efficiency estimate. METR time horizons OpenAI’s current coding-agent evidence is narrower but directly relevant: leaner configurations improved evaluation scores about 10–15% while using 41–66% fewer total tokens. OpenAI model guidance Together, these results support the dashboard’s 1–10× sensitivity range while leaving 1× as the neutral assumption.

Hardware evidence: the series uses current public vendor specifications and public inference benchmarks to normalize a fixed large-model workload across accelerator generations. The plotted values are modeled comparisons rather than direct measurements from operating data centers.

TPU 8i: Google reports up to 2× performance per watt versus Ironwood, so the public-claim scenario uses half Ironwood's energy per token. TPU 8i is still listed as coming soon. TPU 8 deep dive · availability
